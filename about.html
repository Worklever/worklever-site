About

The honest guide to AI tools for small business

Worklever exists because most tool advice is written to sell you the tool. Ours is written so a plumber, a bookkeeper or a shop owner can decide in five minutes — including deciding not to buy.

Why we can afford to be honest

Most review sites can't say a tool is bad — they'd lose the affiliate deal or the sponsorship. We picked a different model on purpose: no sponsored posts, no pay-for-placement, ever. Some links are affiliate links (disclosed every time), but we routinely recommend free tiers and tools that pay us nothing, and every verdict names who should skip the tool — including tools we hold affiliate links for. If the recommendations stop being right, this whole thing stops working. That's the business model keeping us honest.

Who's behind it

Worklever is a small independent research operation, a brand of Strata, run by its owner with AI doing the heavy lifting: our narration uses an AI-generated voice and our visuals are digitally produced, while the research, editorial judgments and every published verdict are human-directed and human-reviewed. We're researchers, not testers — here's exactly how the research works.

What to expect

Four content series (AI for the Trades, The Solo Stack, Main Street AI, The Fine Print), the free AI Stack Builder, and twice-weekly letters once the first episodes ship. Questions? We answer email.
