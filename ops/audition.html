Fliki Voice Audition — instructions for the operating agent

Goal: render ONE test scene in THREE candidate voices so the owner can pick Worklever's permanent brand voice. Use PREVIEW, not export, wherever possible to conserve credits. Note the account's credit balance before starting and report it.

The audition text (paste as the scene script, identical for all three)

Zapier and Make can build the exact same automation and bill you completely differently for it. Here is the part almost every comparison gets backwards. Make counts more things than Zapier does. It charges for the trigger, and it charges every time it checks — even when there is nothing there. That sounds like the expensive one. But on published entry pricing, a single Make credit costs roughly one forty-fourth of a single Zapier task. More units, far cheaper units. Which one actually wins depends entirely on the shape of your workflow, and you can work it out in about ten minutes.

Voice spec (what to look for in the voice library)

VOICE: ElevenLabs, single narrator, US English, mid-range warm male or female — pick one and lock it across the 5-format rotation for channel identity. Style 'conversational documentary', NOT 'energetic explainer'. Stability 55–60, similarity 75, speed 0.97 (the arithmetic scenes need room to land). ASPECT: 16:9, 1080p minimum. PACING: target 145 wpm.

Steps

1From the Fliki dashboard: create a New file → Video → 16:9 landscape → blank/start from script. Name it: Worklever — Voice Audition.
2Paste the audition text above as the first scene's script.
3Open the voice picker. Filter: English (US). Choose a first candidate matching the spec (conversational, mid-range, warm — ElevenLabs-powered voices preferred if available on this plan). Preview/play the scene. Note the exact voice name.
4Repeat the preview with a SECOND and THIRD candidate voice (one can be a different gender for contrast). Note their exact names.
5Report back to the owner: the three voice names tried, any plan-limit warnings encountered, and the credit balance before/after. Do NOT export/render video files in this audition, and do not change any account settings.
